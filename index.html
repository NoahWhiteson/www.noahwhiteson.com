Safesight

Safesight scans room photos for possible safety hazards and suggests practical fixes. It highlights fire, trip, electrical, water, and blocked-exit risks, with explanations of what to check. Review scan history, mark hazards as fixed or dismissed, and track your House Score. Built for iOS with SwiftUI and Google Gemini. It helps you know what to check, rather than replacing a professional inspection.

Undersphere

Undersphere is a browser FPS set inside a spherical world, with gravity that follows you. Walk around the curved surface, find your bearings, and fight with an AK-47, shotgun, or grenades. The world changes how you move, aim, and think about up and down. It won Most Unique at levelsio’s Vibe Jam. Built with TypeScript and Three.js, it runs directly in your browser.

Read more

Volunteen

Volunteen helps students find volunteer opportunities and keep a verified record of their hours. Discover somewhere to help, choose an available shift, and book your time in one place. Your hour records stay alongside your bookings, so you can follow what you’ve contributed without piecing together separate notes. Built with React, TypeScript, Firebase, and Mapbox, with GSAP and Lenis for the website’s motion.

Read more

GoSteer

GoSteer is a crypto wallet for Solana and Ethereum that you use through chat. Check your holdings, work through a proposed transaction, and review the details before sending it. The conversation helps prepare the action step by step, while keeping the final review in your hands. Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Supabase, with Motion for the interface animations.

Read more

Storebase

Storebase is file storage you can run on your own server, with accounts, uploads, and sharing. Keep documents, photos, and other files together in a familiar browser with folders and search. Organise what you upload and share files with others while choosing where your storage runs. Built with React, TypeScript, and Vite on the frontend, and Node.js, Hono, and WebSockets on the server.
